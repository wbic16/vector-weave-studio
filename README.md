# Vector Weave Studio

Vector Weave Studio is designed to help organize your digital assets. It leverages Phext and SQ to give you a generative space that aligns with your artistic vision.

You won't need to worry about filenames - just describe the content in your own terms, and VWS will choose a coordinate that aligns with your vision. This coordinate can later be found via the Gallery, a zoomable interface for exploring your assets.
